## Meeting Notes

```markdown
---
title: "Meeting: [Topic] — [Date]"
tags: [meeting, project-name]
date: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS
---

## Attendees
- Person A
- Person B

## Agenda
1. Item one
2. Item two

## Discussion
- Key point raised...

## Action Items
- [ ] Task 1 — @person — due date
- [ ] Task 2 — @person — due date

## Next Steps
- Follow-up meeting on...
```
